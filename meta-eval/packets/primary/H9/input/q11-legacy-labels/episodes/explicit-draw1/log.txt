## Field definitions (speaker-explicit naming)

In this export, every field is named for the party who authored or
performed it, followed by the party it is directed to. For example,
`sender_message_to_partner` is the sender's message to the partner: it was
authored by SENDER and addressed to PARTNER.

| Field | Authored / performed by | Directed to | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sender_message_to_partner` | SENDER | PARTNER | A message written by SENDER and sent to PARTNER. Any promise in this field is SENDER's promise. |
| `partner_message_to_sender` | PARTNER | SENDER | A message written by PARTNER and sent to SENDER. Any promise in this field is PARTNER's promise. |
| `sender_action_to_partner` | SENDER | PARTNER | An action (SHARE or HOLD) performed by SENDER toward PARTNER. |
| `partner_action_to_sender` | PARTNER | SENDER | An action (SHARE or HOLD) performed by PARTNER toward SENDER. |

The event log in each transcript refers to entries by these field names
only. To determine who promised or did anything, map the field name
through the table above.

action was performed after the condition was met, or when no conditional
promise was activated and broken.
