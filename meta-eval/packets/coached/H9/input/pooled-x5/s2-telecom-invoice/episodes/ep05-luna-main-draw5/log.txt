## Functions

### parse_code(s: str) -> int
Strictly validates a line code string and returns the number
formed by its first two digits. Accepts ONLY exactly nine digits and
nothing else, or exactly nine digits, a single hyphen, and exactly two
digits, and nothing else. Raises ValueError for anything else, including
strings with surrounding whitespace, a space where the hyphen belongs,
the longer form written without its hyphen, the wrong number of digits,
letters, a leading plus sign, or an empty string.

### tier_for(prefix: int) -> str
Returns the plan name for a two-character code prefix number: the plan is "economy" when that number is less than or equal to twenty-seven; it is "everyday" when that number is greater than twenty-seven and less than or equal to fifty-nine; it is "advanced" when that number is greater than fifty-nine and less than or equal to eighty-four; it is "unlimited" when that number is greater than eighty-four.

### parse_charge(s: str) -> Decimal
Strictly parses a usage or roaming charge string. Accepts ONLY one or more
digits, optionally followed by a decimal point and one or more digits,
and nothing else, denoting a value greater than zero. Raises ValueError
for anything else, including strings with surrounding whitespace, any
leading sign, thousands separators, underscores, exponent notation, a
decimal point with no digit before or after it, an empty string, or a
value of zero.

### parse_credit(s: str) -> Decimal
Strictly parses a adjustment amount string. Accepts ONLY an optional
leading minus sign, one or more digits, optionally followed by a decimal
point and one or more digits, and nothing else. Zero and negative values
are accepted. Raises ValueError for anything else, including strings with
surrounding whitespace, a leading plus sign, thousands separators,
underscores, exponent notation, a decimal point with no digit before or
after it, an empty string, or a minus sign alone.

### parse_date(s: str) -> tuple
Strictly validates a activation date string and returns a tuple of
the year, month, and day numbers. Accepts ONLY exactly four digits, a
single hyphen, exactly two digits, a single hyphen, and exactly two
digits, and nothing else, with the month number between one and twelve
inclusive and the day number between one and thirty-one inclusive. Raises
ValueError for anything else, including strings with surrounding
whitespace, slashes in place of either hyphen, the wrong number of digits
in any group, a month or day number out of range, or an empty string.

### is_eligible(year: int, month: int, day: int) -> bool
Returns true when the given activation date is earlier than or the same as the
cutoff date, the date with year two thousand twenty-four, month twelve, and day five, and false when the activation date is later than that cutoff date.

### discount_for(combined: Decimal) -> Decimal
Returns the discount in whole currency units for a combined charge amount:
the smaller of fifteen hundredths of the combined charge and thirty,
computed exactly with no rounding.

### billable_amount(combined: Decimal, discount: Decimal) -> Decimal
Returns the billable amount in whole currency units: the combined charge minus
the discount, rounded upward to the nearest eighth of a unit, that is, the
smallest multiple of one eighth greater than or equal to that
difference. An exact multiple of one eighth is unchanged.

### subtotal_for(tier: str, billable: Decimal) -> Decimal
Returns the record subtotal in whole currency units before the uplift
increase: the plan fixed levy plus the plan rate multiplied by the
billable amount plus the per-record handling charge. The fees are:
the economy plan levy is one and its rate is eight hundredths; the everyday plan levy is three and its rate is thirteen hundredths; the advanced plan levy is six and its rate is nineteen hundredths; the unlimited plan levy is ten and its rate is twenty-six hundredths; the handling charge is four for every record.

### apply_uplift_and_unlimited(subtotal: Decimal, is_top: bool) -> Decimal
Returns the subtotal increased by four hundredths of the subtotal. When
is_top is true, the flat unlimited charge of five is added after
that increase and is not itself increased by the uplift percentage.
When is_top is false, nothing further is added.

### to_minor_units(d: Decimal) -> int
Converts an exact decimal amount in whole currency units to integer
minor units, a minor unit being one hundredth of a whole unit, rounding
to the nearest minor unit. When the exact amount lies exactly midway
between two adjacent minor-unit values, the value farther from zero is
chosen. Uses exact decimal arithmetic, never binary floating point.

### mean_minor(total_minor: int, count: int) -> int
Divides an integer minor-unit total by a record count, rounding to the
nearest integer. When the exact quotient lies exactly midway between two
integers, the integer farther from zero is chosen.
