## Features (fixed definitions; use exactly these)

Extract exactly these six features from a candidate answer. Judge each feature only from that answer's own text together with the Problem it answers.

- `f_core` (0, 1, or 2): 2 means the answer states a complete construction or procedure that addresses every constraint stated in the Problem. 1 means the answer states a partial construction or procedure that addresses some but not all of the stated constraints, or leaves part of the construction unspecified. 0 means the answer states no construction or procedure at all.
- `f_check` (0 or 1): 1 means the answer reports a check actually applied to its own proposal, with an outcome stated as a specific value or observed result. A check that is only proposed for the future counts as `f_next`, not `f_check`. A generic assurance that the proposal works, with no applied check and no stated outcome, is `f_check`=0.
- `f_compare` (0 or 1): 1 means the answer compares at least two alternatives and gives a reason for choosing one over another. Merely naming two alternatives with no reason for the choice is `f_compare`=0.
- `f_contra` (0 or 1): 1 means the answer states or uses something that contradicts a constraint explicitly stated in the Problem (for example using a material, tool, or method the Problem excludes). Otherwise `f_contra`=0.
- `f_limit` (0 or 1): 1 means the answer states a limitation of its proposal or a condition under which the proposal fails. Merely leaving part of the work unfinished is not a limitation statement, so it is `f_limit`=0 by itself.
- `f_next` (0 or 1): 1 means the answer proposes a specific next step or future test, naming what would be done. A generic intention such as planning to think more about it, with no specific step named, is `f_next`=0.

The score is an ordinal from 0 to 3.
