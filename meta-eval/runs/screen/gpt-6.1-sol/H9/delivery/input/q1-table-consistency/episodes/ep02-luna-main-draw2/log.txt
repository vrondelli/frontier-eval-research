Candidate answer:

A fixed 8 centimetre gap would jam fruit that is nearly 8 centimetres wide, so I choose the widening slot over a fixed gap because borderline fruit settles before it can jam. This design will obviously sort every round fruit correctly, so its correctness is clear from the geometry.
