Title: Automatic quarantine for repeatedly flaky tests
We keep a rolling 14-day record for each test. If a test both passes and fails on
the same commit in at least 3 of the last 20 runs, we move it to a quarantine
suite that still runs nightly but does not block merges. A ticket is filed
automatically. The test leaves quarantine after 20 consecutive passes.
Test of the idea: turn it on for one repository for two weeks and count blocked
merges before and after. Cost: one nightly job.
