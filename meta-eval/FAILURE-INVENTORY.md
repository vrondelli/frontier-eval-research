# Hackathon failure inventory for an eval-author evaluation

8 November 2026. This is a second, read-only synthesis of the [46-package directory-level audit](../complete-recap-audit-20261108/REPORT.md), checked independently in three lanes against selected primary reports and receipts. **The repeated eval-building workflow did not consistently produce a sound eval, despite many attempts and accumulating evidence.** This inventory documents the concrete failure families and counted occurrences. It does not semantically regrade every archived program. The 45 early top-level packages include designs, exports and duplicate reports; the frontier package contains overlapping discovery, confirmation and cross-model cuts. An older inventory indexes 550 upstream reports but has not semantically adjudicated all of them. No defensible global numerator or denominator exists. Numbers below retain their study and unit; related rows can overlap. The [replication design](DESIGN.md) reuses the original jobs to test fresh GPT eval authors directly.

**Labels:** `A` = defect in our AI-assisted eval-authoring or audit workflow (A17 specifically concerns a subject-authored self-validator); `S` = observed subject-model task/process error; `P` = authored probe showing a possible failure, with **zero observed subject occurrences**; `U` = unresolved or condition-limited. Rows A14 and A15 are `A/P`: they prove weaknesses in an authored grader using synthetic controls, not observed GPT cheating. An `A` label does not mean a controlled experiment has already isolated GPT as the sole author: the workflow also involved human direction, iterative revision and later audits. The proposed experiment in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) isolates the eval author.

## Coverage boundary and count rules

This inventory covers all 46 top-level research packages at directory/protocol level and verifies selected primary sources, receipts and adversarial controls. It does **not** semantically regrade every artifact in those packages or the 550 indexed upstream reports; unadjudicated archive rows therefore have no claim of zero failures. One row means one **failure family**, not one independent model trial. A single artifact may be both a subject error and a false-green grader observation. Never sum tables A and B. Report the frozen registered primary, broad delivered-goal correctness, eligible exposure, invalid/unknown, and posthoc findings in separate fields. Keep discovery, same-artifact replay, fresh same-condition replication and changed-condition intervention separate. A local promotion threshold is a recurrence decision, not proof that a documented error did not happen.
