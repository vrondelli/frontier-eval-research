# Claim and measurement contract

The subject is a GPT coding agent asked to create an evaluation package for an original hackathon job. The historical evidence establishes repeated failures in an AI-assisted eval-authoring workflow. New trials measure fresh author outputs; they do not retrospectively establish an isolated causal model effect in that workflow.

Each attempt is one new session, one source packet and one construction budget. The submitted package is immutable after terminal completion. Owner grading is offline and cannot feed repairs into that attempt.

Instruction conditions are `exact_original`, `minimal_reconstruction`, `reconstructed_adapter` and `coached_secondary`. Exact requires recovered authoring text and evidence of the original accessible materials. A downstream subject prompt alone is insufficient. Any newly required `grade.py` or JSON transport is an adapter addition and must be labeled, even if its instruction is short. A coached checklist is a separate secondary arm.

Measure runnable delivery, reference acceptance, wrong-candidate rejection, public-contract fidelity, actual-work measurement, oracle isolation, complete process/source accounting and supported final claims. Record per-check `pass`, `fail`, `unknown` or `review_required`. A missing qualified check cannot become a pass. Semantic/manual cases cannot be converted into a failure by a private interpretation.

Controls derived from historical programs, newly authored mutants and simulated logs have separate provenance. A passing synthetic exploit demonstrates a grader weakness; it is not an observed model cheating event. An evaluator that detects a known witness but misses a fresh sibling has incomplete mechanism coverage. Successful formal outputs remain successful even when other models or historical attempts fail.

The final report must keep case, model, instruction condition, attempt, timeout and eligible-exposure denominators separate. No pooled global reliability rate is justified. No frontier limitation is claimed without actual stronger-model evidence on this eval-authoring task.
