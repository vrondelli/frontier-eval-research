# Conclusions and remaining questions

## Supported findings

1. **Real errors recur.** Lying-helper failures recur across Luna cuts. A payroll bug recurs in a fresh Sol output. Saved-artifact numeric and grader failures reproduce; fresh-generation outcomes vary.
2. **Model-created evals can fail their construction goal.** H1 adds an undisclosed success requirement. H6 misses a real program defect and rejects equivalent legal output. These are concrete defects in delivered evaluators.
3. **Several apparent model failures came from our instruments.** Private exception types, hidden vocabulary, invalid controls and incomplete discovery do not become legitimate failure evidence by repetition.
4. **Successful follow-ups matter.** Explicit repair passes 30/30; stronger-model query comparison passes eight per model; warehouse pilots and the original-five initial comparison pass. Some original failures do not recur.
5. **Conditions explain apparent inconsistency.** Tools, clarification, scope, reporting exposure and promotion thresholds change the question. No promoted hit is weaker than no task error.
6. **A runnable meta-eval exists.** It executes saved packages, scores with explicit unknowns, exercises adversarial calibration and supports clean replay without new model calls.

## Not established

No population failure rate, universal frontier limitation, deliberate gaming, online training on these runs or comprehensive absence of leakage is established. Aliases do not pin model weights. Shared AI assistance does not supply external human ground truth.

Finite success and high partial scores do not prove full-domain trustworthiness. These tasks do not validate human creativity or employment-selection use.

## Remaining unknowns

- Human semantic review is incomplete. Five H1 source-rule approvals do not approve every outer-grader judgment.
- Historical generation read isolation is not established comprehensively; narrow probes and zero path hits are not confinement.
- Some references/positive controls cover only finite subsets. Large nonzero probes can exceed our checker budget; that is an owner unknown.
- Measurement applicability, source fidelity and qualitative conclusions need task-specific review. Executable software cannot be retroactively required of a paper-rubric task.
- H1 budget draw3 has an intermittent worker-protocol rejection. Both primary and clean-replay outcomes remain preserved.

## Most useful next experiment

Freeze new complete-eval authoring tasks before inference, with independent human-qualified obligations and private owner checks. Keep the model, prompt, tools and runtime fixed. Separate source fidelity, correct-answer acceptance, fault rejection and report honesty. Retain a fixed sample, then replicate under a declared policy.

That would test generalization. The current scored dataset remains retrospective evidence and software, with explicit limits.
