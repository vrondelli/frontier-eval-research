## Failures actually observed

No formal failures occurred in this run. The revised suite did not establish frontier failure cases; successful answers are retained. Synthetic negative controls are evaluator tests, not model failures.

## Scope

One episode per alias/task cannot estimate a model population failure rate. Formal passes do not validate novelty, physical execution, human creativity or future engineering performance. Candidate execution/format mistakes, transport errors, semantic counterexamples and task/grader defects are separate classes; no failure should be marketed as meaningful solely because a check is red.

Preparation and attribution/link-support errata are disclosed in preparation notes (historical reference: `PREPARATION-NOTES.md`). Frozen evaluator/prompt inputs were not changed after inference began. No participant data was collected and no event submission was made.
