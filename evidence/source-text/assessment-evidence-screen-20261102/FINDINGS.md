## What this tells us about the direction

This finite suite has an observed ceiling on the tested aliases. It currently supplies useful positive/boundary controls and a regression harness, **not evidence that frontier models fail at these six tasks**. Authored negative controls that fail the grader are not model failures. A clear all-pass result satisfies this experiment's stop rule; we did not enlarge numbers,alter contracts or sample until a failure appeared.

A design limitation is that the public tasks supply the relevant world model: transitions,coverage sets,compatible interpretations,possible completions,profile targets and question outcomes. They test reasoning within that representation. They do not test whether an agent can derive a defensible representation or useful criteria from messy developer answers,source code,incidents or conflicting practitioner evidence. That distinction identifies an untested question,not a causally demonstrated explanation for the ceiling.

The earlier wording/ambiguity findings are not replaced or resolved by this screen. Different tasks and disclosure conditions prevent a causal claim that formalization generally fixes scoring. This experiment also does not test broad creative ability,sincerity,cooperation in practice,real developer performance,long-horizon engineering,or the three-answer hiring thesis. Human relevance and validation remain pending as requested.

## Research disposition proposed to the owner

Keep the six families as calibration/regression controls. Do not select them as the hackathon's central failure tasks on the present evidence. A new discovery study should target extracting and defending criteria/world models from genuine engineering artifacts,with independently defensible certificates and practitioner review. That is a successor hypothesis requiring its own design and freeze; it is not an unrun result of this study or an already approved owner decision.

No hackathon submission,public publication,participant recruitment,recording or employment decision occurred. The first-place objective remains open even though this experimental direction now has a clear negative result.
