# Boundary repairs, N/A and fresh full-batch results

Completed 36 native gpt-6.1-sol/high calls through Codex app-server, with 474/474 saved judgments. Comparison/adoption/assertion repairs and all fresh cases/references were peer-reviewed and fixed before inference. No trial or repair was selected from the outcomes.

## What changed

Comparison requires a candidate-conveyed distinct-course choice plus a supported reason; a route's own drawback or guard is not enough. A conveyed comparison with an essential unverified premise is unknown, while known route+limitation can still retain 2. Adoption requires a condition/finding linked to a decision or claim, rather than activity sequencing. A7 zero requires a contradicted factual/guarantee assertion; unsafe imperatives alone do not supply one. Compatible source readings with the same A7 category can be scored while action polarity remains unresolved.

The schema now has N/A, required applicability and applicability_basis. Known absence of opportunity is N/A; unresolved applicability is U; known-applicable missing answer is U. N/A and unknown applicability use empty features. Optional E3 gate uncertainty does not automatically force overall U. The shared contract excludes N/A/U from ordinal aggregates. Both model arms receive this new contract; only the supplied instrument differs within each matched pair.
