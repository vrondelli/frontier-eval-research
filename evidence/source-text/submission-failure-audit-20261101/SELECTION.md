## Relevance and originality

**Diagnosis:** choosing a test can consume the resources needed to resolve uncertainty. The saved failures represent current possibilities correctly but make poor information-budget allocations. Their ablations do not show removal of another necessary probe causing failure. Production incident response is an analogy; transfer to real debugging or long-horizon coding has not been demonstrated, and the practical controls pass.

The specific instance tables, traces, earliest-loss witnesses and replay package are original project artifacts. Bounded-lie search and cost-sensitive diagnosis are established. [Last-Chance Policy Identification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36741) closely covers irreversible diagnostic resources and feasibility boundaries. Its recovery-policy homogenization differs from identifying our fixed initial world, but a claim that we discovered the last-safe-choice principle would be indefensible. Original eval instances do not require a newly invented scientific principle; the contribution must be stated accurately.

**Depot construction:** one feasible job is left unreleased because the old answer misses release-first optimization. A six-job witness proves that error without trusting the optimizer. This is meaningful finite plan construction inspired by function-preserving redesign. Scheduling and functional-fixedness research already exist; correctness or missed throughput does not validate a general creativity score. [NoveltyBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.05228) measures diverse useful generations, a different target from our closed finite optimization.
